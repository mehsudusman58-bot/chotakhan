Saaf, sehatmand skin ke liye ek sacha rasta.

Chota Khan aapko wo cheez batata hai jo mehngi creamein nahi batatin — ke acne sirf skin ka masla nahi, jo aap khate ho uska bhi asar hota hai.

7 mahine Treatment ke baad bhi agar acne wapas aa raha hai, to wajah shayad bahar nahi, andar hai.

Acne sirf face wash se theek nahi hota

Teen cheezein hain jo aksar nazar andaz hoti hain jab log sirf products badalte rehte hain.

Khana

Fast food, oily aur sugar wali cheezein oil glands ko active kar deti hain aur hormonal acne ko badhati hain.

Neend

7-8 ghante ki poori neend na lena skin ko repair hone ka time nahi deta — pimples zyada der tak rehte hain.

Andar ki Health

Triglycerides ya thyroid jaisi cheezein bhi skin par asar dalti hain — kabhi kabhi masla sirf blood test se pata chalta hai.

Roz ka routine, 4 qadam mein

Koi shortcut nahi — bas consistency chahiye.

01

Halka face wash

Subah aur raat, sirf 2 baar. Baar baar dhona skin ko aur oily bana deta hai.

02

Sunscreen, har din

Bina sunscreen ke acne ke nishaan (marks) aur gehre ho jate hain, chahe dhoop kam ho ya zyada.

03

Khane mein tabdeeli

Fast food dheere dheere kam karo — ek dum chhodna mushkil hai, hafte mein ek baar se shuru karo.

04

Sabr aur waqt

Skin ko badalne mein kam se kam 3-4 hafte lagte hain — roz check karne se farak nazar nahi aata.

Jitna ho sake, in cheezon se parhez

— Burger, fries, aur tali hui cheezein
— Cold drinks aur zyada sugar wali cheezein
— Raat gaye khana, sone se pehle
— Baar baar face ko haath lagana
— Bina wajah naye products aazmana
— Kam neend, zyada stress
